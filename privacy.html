Where it goes

When you submit the form it is sent to a function running on our hosting provider, which composes an email and delivers it to our inbox. In order, the information passes through:

ProcessorRoleWhere
VercelHosts this site and runs the form handler. Keeps standard server logs (IP address, user agent, requested URL) for operational and security purposes.United States
Resend (via Amazon SES)Delivers the enquiry email to us.us-east-1, United States
CloudflareDNS for this domain, and forwards mail sent to our address to the inbox we read.Global

The submission is not written to any database. The only lasting copies are the emails in the studio's mailboxes — the shared address and a direct copy to the person who answers enquiries.

Cookies and analytics

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. There is no tracking pixel, no advertising tag, and no third-party script that profiles you. Nothing is stored in your browser.

Fonts

The typefaces on this site (Space Grotesk, Space Mono and Instrument Serif) are served from this domain, not from Google Fonts. Loading a page therefore makes no request to any third-party font host, and your IP address is not disclosed to one.

Spam protection

The form includes a hidden field that real visitors never see and a check on how quickly it was submitted. Both exist to reject automated submissions. We also limit how many submissions one network address can make in a minute. No third-party CAPTCHA is used and nothing about you is sent to a bot-detection vendor.
