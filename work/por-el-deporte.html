Most neighbourhood football clubs die in the garage. A crest gets drawn, a batch of shirts gets ordered, and the wrong sizes sit in boxes until someone gives them away. The culture is real enough — the Sunday game, the group chat, the people who keep turning up — but the thing you can actually buy is usually an afterthought. Por El Deporte has been putting on games in Key Biscayne since 2014. The shirt is how the club travels.

Por El Deporte is a Miami soccer apparel brand and a club that still plays. The storefront carries the "Palmas" jersey, a hoodie, tropical tees, hats and totes — made to order, free US shipping, about a week to the door — and says plainly on the product page that every order helps fund the next game. One codebase serves the browser and installs to the home screen on iOS and Android, so there is no second app drifting out of step.

The site is built like a matchday programme, not a catalogue. Cream sand, crest orange, sea blue, pitch green. A fat display face set enormous over plain Montserrat. Cards and buttons outlined in ink and dropped onto a solid orange offset, so the page reads as screen print rather than software. The photography does the rest: keepers stretched low, families on the touchline, shirts worn in the weather they were made for.

“The shirt is how the club travels.”
