AGENT PLATFORM

Agos

An agentic operating system for institutional asset management — one governed state for the team and its agents.

THE PROBLEM

Investment decisions rest on evidence, relationships, models and prior judgement scattered across a firm’s existing systems, so the reasoning behind a position is reconstructed from memory rather than read.

WHAT WE BUILT

A platform that connects those systems into one governed graph and puts agents to work on top of it, with every action, review and history entry tied back to its source.
