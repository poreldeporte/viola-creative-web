COMMERCE

Champy's Seafood

A Miami seafood business selling stone crab and prepared food online, on a storefront it can run itself.

THE PROBLEM

A seasonal product, a phone number, and no way to take an order online — every sale needed a person on the other end of the line, and the season is short.

WHAT WE BUILT

A storefront built around the season: the catch front and centre, the menu and merch shoppable, and ordering that works at two in the morning without anybody picking up.
