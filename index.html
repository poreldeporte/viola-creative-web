(THE OBVIOUS QUESTIONS)

The questions we always get.

What kinds of projects do you take on?+

Websites, web apps, mobile apps, and the design and discovery work around them — plus agent workflows where they genuinely help. Most projects are a few months with a small senior team. If something is outside what we do well, we will say so and often point you elsewhere.

Can you work alongside our existing team?+

Usually yes — we often work with an in-house developer, a marketing team or an existing designer. We are explicit up front about who owns which decisions so nobody is guessing, and we document as we go so your team can carry it.

What happens when an agent gets it wrong?+

Agents work inside limits you set, and anything consequential waits for a human approval. Every action is logged and reversible, and you can pause any agent instantly. We start them on low-stakes work and widen the remit only once you trust it.

Do we own the code and the design files?+

Yes — all of it, assigned to you in the contract. Code in your repository, design files in your workspace, everything running in your accounts. It is how we prefer to work, not an upsell.

We already have systems. Do we start over?+

Almost never. We usually rebuild the one or two things causing the most friction, integrate cleanly with what is working, and leave the rest alone. Discovery tells us which is which before you commit to anything.
